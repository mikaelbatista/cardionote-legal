CardioNote Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

CardioNote is a wellness app for keeping a blood pressure log, estimating your pulse with the phone camera, and preparing a report for your doctor. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

CardioNote is provided by Mikael Batista da Trindade ("we", "us"). You can reach us at cardionote8@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your health data stays on your phone. We do not have a server, and we never receive it.
  • You do not create an account.
  • The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Blood pressure readings, pulse estimates, heart rate variability and stress estimates
  • Blood glucose and weight records
  • Medications, their schedules, and whether you marked each dose as taken or skipped
  • Notes and tags you add
  • Profiles you create (a name, an optional year of birth and a color)
  • Reminders and app settings
  • Your answers to the optional questions on first launch (your goal, age range, and whether you have a blood pressure monitor)

We cannot see, access or recover this information. If you delete the app or use "Delete all data" in the app, it is removed from your device.

Camera

When you estimate your pulse, the app uses the back camera and the flash. Each camera frame is reduced to an average color value on your device and then discarded. No photo or video is saved, and nothing from the camera leaves your phone.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. The app connects only if you tap "Connect Apple Health" and grant permission.

  • Reading: with your permission, the app reads blood pressure, heart rate, resting heart rate, weight and blood glucose from Apple Health and copies them into your log on your device.
  • Writing: if you turn it on, the app saves the blood pressure, weight and blood glucose readings you enter to Apple Health. Pulse and heart rate variability estimates made with the camera are never written to Apple Health.

Information obtained from Apple Health is used only to show your records in the app. It is not used for advertising or marketing, it is not sold, and it is not shared with any third party. It is not stored in iCloud by the app.

You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in the iPhone Settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. We do not send notifications from a server. A medication reminder shows the medication name you typed so that you know which dose it refers to.

Sharing you choose to do

The app lets you create a PDF report, a CSV file, a weekly summary and a password-protected backup file. These are created on your device. They leave your phone only when you choose to send or save them, and they go only where you send them. Once you share a file, it is handled by the app or service you chose.

Device backups

Like most apps, CardioNote's data may be included in the backups of your iPhone that you set up with Apple (for example iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer). Those backups are managed by you and by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The app itself does not use iCloud to store or sync your health data.

Purchases and subscriptions

CardioNote is currently free and has no purchases, so the app does not send any information to a subscription service today. This section describes what will happen if a paid subscription (CardioNote Premium) is offered in the future. If you subscribe, the payment is handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address.

To know whether a subscription is active, the app would use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat would receive purchase information (such as the product purchased, purchase and expiration dates, and whether a trial is active), a random identifier created for the app installation, and technical information such as your device type, operating system version, app version, country and IP address. RevenueCat would not receive any health data. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, non-identifying statistics and crash reports about the app. These do not include your health records.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics software in the app.
  • We do not build a profile of you.

Children

CardioNote is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because your records are stored only on your device, you control them directly:

  • See or correct your data: open the app and edit or delete any record.
  • Get a copy: use the PDF, CSV or backup features.
  • Delete everything: use "Delete all data" in the app, or delete the app.
  • Apple Health: turn permissions on or off in the Health app.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under local law, such as the right to know what personal information a business holds about you and to ask for it to be deleted. We do not hold your health records. For any question or request about the limited purchase information described above, contact us at cardionote8@gmail.com.

Security

Your records are kept in the app's private storage on your device and are protected by the security of your iPhone, including your passcode. Backup files you create are encrypted with the password you choose. We cannot recover that password. No method of storage is completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your device and your backup files safe.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
